GARDEN OF JIHAN — جيهان
Find the moments worth sharing

START IN THREE STEPS

1. Right-click the downloaded ZIP and choose Extract All.
   Do not open Garden of Jihan from inside the ZIP preview.

2. Open the extracted folder and double-click:
   START GARDEN OF JIHAN.cmd

3. Wait a moment. Garden of Jihan opens automatically.
   Keep the extracted folder together; there is nothing to install.

READY FROM THE FIRST VIDEO

Everything needed to understand your videos is already included. Long videos
show their progress and time remaining while the garden listens. Later videos
start just as simply.

PRIVATE AND FREE

- Video understanding and clip creation happen on this computer.
- No account, administrator access, subscription, credits, or payment is required.
- A connection is used only when you choose an online video or decide to share.

ENDING A SESSION

Use Close Garden in the upper-right corner. Saved projects remain on this
computer. To reopen, double-click START GARDEN OF JIHAN.cmd again.

IF WINDOWS ASKS FOR CONFIRMATION

Extract the ZIP before opening it and accept only a copy received directly from
the Garden of Jihan maintainer.

Process and republish only media you have permission to use.
