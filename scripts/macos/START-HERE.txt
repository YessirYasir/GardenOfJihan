Garden of Jihan — جيهان

GET STARTED
1. Move “Garden of Jihan” to your Applications folder if you would like to keep it there.
2. Open Garden of Jihan.
3. Your usual browser opens the private Garden of Jihan dashboard.
4. Paste a supported video address or choose a video from your Mac.
5. Choose the language and clip settings, then select Find My Moments.
6. Preview, adjust, keep, and export your clips.

Everything needed to understand your videos is included. Nothing else needs to be installed.
A visible clock shows how long the current video has been processing and the estimated time remaining.

Your video work stays on this Mac. No account, subscription, credits, or payment is required.
Only a finished video you explicitly choose to publish can leave the private garden.

Process and republish only media you have permission to use.
